### September 21, 2026

## files

When you export (download) a Sheet or Workbook as CSV, non-ASCII characters now display
correctly in Excel and Google Sheets. Accented names such as "José", curly quotes, and
non-Latin scripts such as Japanese are no longer garbled.

Previously, Excel and Google Sheets guessed the wrong text encoding, so "José" showed as
"JosÃ©". The exported CSV now begins with a marker that tells those applications to read
it as UTF-8.

This applies to CSV exports only and happens automatically. There is no setting to change.
