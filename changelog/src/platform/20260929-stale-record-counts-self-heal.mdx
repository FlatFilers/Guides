## dataxp

**Stuck Record Counts Now Correct Themselves**

If a Sheet's record counts fall behind its latest changes, Flatfile now detects this and recalculates the counts automatically, usually within a few minutes. Previously, an interrupted count update could leave a Sheet's counts stuck at an outdated total indefinitely. For example, after a large mapping import, a Sheet could keep showing every record as an error.
