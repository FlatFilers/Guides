## dataxp

**Reliable Rollback for Failed and Canceled Large Imports**

We've improved how large, multi-part imports recover when they fail or are canceled. Previously, such an import could strand partial records in the destination Sheet. It could also leave the import unable to be finalized, keeping the Submit step blocked.

Now the rollback runs reliably to completion. A failed or canceled large import leaves no leftover records, reports accurate record counts, and no longer blocks you from finalizing and submitting your import.
