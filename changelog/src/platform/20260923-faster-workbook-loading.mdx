### September 23, 2026

## spaces

**Faster Workbook Loading in Spaces with Many Workbooks**

Listing the Workbooks in a Space could previously slow down as the number of Workbooks grew. Each Workbook was prepared one at a time before the list could return. The platform now prepares several Workbooks at once, so the list loads noticeably faster in Spaces that contain many Workbooks. Spaces with only a few Workbooks were already fast and are unaffected, and this improvement applies automatically with nothing for you to configure.
