### September 30, 2026

## dataxp

**Actions no longer stay disabled after large imports with `trackChanges`**

On Workbooks with `trackChanges` enabled, actions and filters are disabled until
all commits are complete. After large imports, some commits could stay open
indefinitely. As a result, the Sheet kept showing "This action will be available
after all hooks finish running" even after your hooks had finished.

This happened when a listener, such as `@flatfile/plugin-record-hook`, tried to
complete a commit before Flatfile had finished recording it. The completion
request returned a 404 error and the commit was never marked complete.

Flatfile now records each commit before it emits the `commit:created` event, so
your listeners can complete the commit as soon as they receive the event. If a
request to `POST /v1/commits/:commitId/complete` arrives before the commit is
available, Flatfile waits up to about 15 seconds for it before returning a 404
error. The same wait applies to commits that don't exist or that you can't
access. Those requests now take about 15 seconds to return a 404 error.

No changes to your listener code are needed.
