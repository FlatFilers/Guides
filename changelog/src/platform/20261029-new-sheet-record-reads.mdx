### October 29, 2026

## core

**Faster Record Reads on Newly Created Sheets**

Fixed an issue where reading records from a newly created Sheet could become very slow. Since October 17, 2026, paged record reads from a new Sheet could slow down for up to three hours after records were first written to it. Large Sheets with unique fields were affected most, especially when reading later pages, and some requests timed out. For example, a job that wrote records and then requested the second page of 10,000 records could fail before finishing. New Sheets now return records at full speed right after their first write, and an import into one Sheet no longer slows reads from the Workbook's other Sheets. You don't need to change anything.
