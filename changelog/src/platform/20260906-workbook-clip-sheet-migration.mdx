### September 6, 2026

## dataxp

**Data Clips keep their data when workbooks migrate to newer storage**

A workbook containing Data Clips could previously fail to migrate to newer storage, or migrate but arrive without the data held in its Data Clips.

With this fix, workbooks that contain Data Clips now complete the migration successfully and move onto newer storage with their Data Clip data preserved. The migration happens automatically and requires no configuration or action on your part.
