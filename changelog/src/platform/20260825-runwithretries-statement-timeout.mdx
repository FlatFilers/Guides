## dataxp

**More Stable Performance Under Heavy Load**

During periods of high database demand, a slow or expensive query behind record and count operations could previously be re-run repeatedly. That repetition compounded the load and prolonged the slowdown. Such queries now stop quickly instead of repeating, so a single expensive query no longer snowballs into wider instability. The result is a more stable, responsive platform under heavy load, with nothing for you to configure or do.
