### September 11, 2026

## dataxp

**Workbook storage migrations recover cleanly after a failed attempt**

When Flatfile moves a Workbook onto newer storage, an attempt can occasionally fail or be interrupted partway through. Previously, the next attempt built on top of the leftover state from the failed one, which could cause retries to keep failing. A Workbook in that state could remain unable to move onto newer storage, though it kept working normally on its existing storage in the meantime.

Now each attempt discards any leftover state from a previous failed attempt and starts fresh, and it waits before retrying instead of failing again right away. When a large Workbook temporarily runs out of room partway through the move — because its newer storage is still growing to fit it — the migration now waits for that space before trying again. Workbooks that previously kept failing to migrate, including large ones, now complete the move successfully. This happens automatically, with nothing to configure or do.
