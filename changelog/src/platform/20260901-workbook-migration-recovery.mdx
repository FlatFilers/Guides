### September 01, 2026

## dataxp

**Your Changes Are Now Protected During Workbook Recovery**

Previously, a workbook could occasionally get stuck and become unavailable, with no way to recover
it yourself short of contacting support. Now it recovers on its own. Simply opening the workbook
again automatically restores access, with no support ticket and no manual step.

Just as important, any changes you save to the workbook during this recovery are preserved and can
no longer be overwritten or discarded. There's nothing you need to configure or do differently.
